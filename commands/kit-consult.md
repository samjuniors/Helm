Follow the consult protocol (kind: feature, fix, compare, research, improve, or build for a whole product from an idea) with the `kit_consult` tool for what I describe after this command (if I wrote nothing, ask me for it in one sentence).
1. `kit_scan`, then `kit_guard` baseline if none exists, then `kit_consult` open with my words.
2. Ask me at most 3 questions, only where the answer would change your recommendation, each with what it changes. Stop and wait. If nothing needs asking, state your assumption and go on.
3. Record graded evidence (Verified with a source, Stable, Inference, Unknown). Look up anything current instead of remembering it; if you cannot, grade it Unknown.
4. Record at least two options with the risk of each to what already works. If I asked to compare products, add facts per criterion with grade and source.
5. Record your recommendation with the strongest argument against it, a verdict and what would change it, and show it to me. Do not plan or build until I agree. I record my agreement in a terminal; tell me the exact command it printed.
6. After I agree: `kit_consult` plan to put the steps in the roadmap, build, run `kit_guard` check after each step, report each round with `kit_consult` round, and obey its CONTINUE, DONE or STOP. When a milestone is done run `kit_state`.
